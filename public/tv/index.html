Where does “Smart Alec” come from?

It’s American slang from the mid-1800s for someone who’s irritatingly clever or cocky.

The favourite story

In 1840s New York, a con man called Alexander “Alec” Hoag ran a trick with his wife, Melinda. She lured men back to a room, and while they were distracted, Alec crept in through a secret panel in the wall and lifted their wallets.

He was so slick he became known as “Smart Alec”. He paid the police to look the other way… until he got too clever, stopped paying them, and was arrested.

The catch

The phrase doesn’t appear in print until the 1860s, and nobody has proved it was named after Hoag. So, like all the best quiz answers, take it with a pinch of salt.

You’ll see it spelled both Smart Alec and Smart Aleck.
